Settings & How to Use in Stremio

📱 How to add this Addon to Stremio:

  1. Click the "Install in App" button at the top of the page.
  2. If the Stremio app doesn't open automatically, click "Copy Link".
  3. In Stremio, go to the Addons (Puzzle icon) tab, paste the URL into the search bar, and press Enter.
  4. Click Install.
  5. Done! On your Stremio home screen (or under Discover), you will see the categories:
    • 🟢 Watchlist: Ready (Good Quality)
    • 📋 Watchlist: All Movies
    • ⏳ Watchlist: Waiting for Quality
Periodically checks in the background whether pending movies have become available in good quality.
